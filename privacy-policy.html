Privacy Policy for Bill Splitter

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bill Splitter ("the App"), developed by Zenvora Labs ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the App.

Bill Splitter helps users manage shared expenses, calculate balances and track settlements. The App uses Google AdMob for advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable advertising privacy choices.

1. Information We Process

Bill Splitter does not require account registration for its core expense-splitting functionality.

Information entered into the App may include:

  • Participant names and group names
  • Expense descriptions and amounts
  • Currency preferences
  • Balances and settlement records
  • App settings and preferences

This expense information is intended to be stored locally on your device and used to provide the App's expense-management features.

Separately, third-party advertising services may automatically collect or process certain device, advertising, and usage information as described below.

2. How Information Is Used

Information entered into Bill Splitter is used to:

  • Calculate shared expenses
  • Display individual balances
  • Calculate settlement amounts
  • Maintain locally stored expense history
  • Remember app preferences

Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners to deliver advertisements, measure performance, prevent fraud, and support advertising operations.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

Bill Splitter integrates Google AdMob, a mobile advertising service provided by Google.

Depending on your device, region, privacy choices and Google's advertising configuration, AdMob may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers, where available
  • Device information and identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it
  • Ad impressions, clicks and interactions
  • App interaction and diagnostic information
  • Information used for fraud prevention and security

Google may use this information for personalized or non-personalized advertising, advertising measurement, security, and related purposes, subject to applicable laws and user choices.

For more information, see: Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob privacy guidance .

4. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Bill Splitter uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request or manage advertising consent where applicable.

Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be presented with a consent message or other advertising privacy options.

Where supported, you can review or update advertising privacy choices through the privacy options provided within the App.

Your device may also provide controls for advertising identifiers and ad personalization.

Consent choices do not necessarily disable all advertising or all processing necessary for security, fraud prevention, and service delivery.

5. Data Sharing

Bill Splitter is not designed to upload your locally stored expense records to Zenvora Labs or advertising providers for advertising purposes.

However, Google AdMob and associated advertising technology providers may receive or process device and advertising-related information.

If you choose to share a settlement summary, the information is transferred through the application or service you select on your device. That service's privacy practices may apply.

We do not sell your locally entered expense records.

6. Payments and Financial Services

Bill Splitter is an expense calculation and record-keeping utility.

It does not directly transfer money, provide loans, connect to bank accounts, offer financial advice, or operate as a digital wallet.

7. Permissions and Device Information

The App does not require access to your contacts, precise GPS location, camera, or microphone for its core expense-splitting functionality.

Advertising services may use network access, device identifiers, and other information permitted by your device settings and applicable platform policies.

8. Data Storage and Security

Expense records and app preferences are intended to remain on your device.

We take reasonable measures within our control to support the security of the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Device backup and restore services may retain copies of locally stored data according to your device and operating system settings.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

You may delete locally stored groups and expenses using available App functionality, clear the App's data through your device settings, or uninstall the App.

Operating-system backups may retain information independently of the App.

Information processed by third-party advertising providers is subject to their respective data retention and deletion practices.

10. Children's Privacy

Bill Splitter is not specifically designed for children. We do not intentionally request personal information from children for the App's core expense-management features.

Advertising and privacy settings must be configured consistently with the App's declared target audience and applicable child privacy laws.

11. Third-Party Services

The App integrates or may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — advertising delivery and measurement
  • Google UMP — advertising consent management
  • Google Play services — platform functionality

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable contractual arrangements.

12. International Data Processing

Third-party advertising providers may process device and advertising-related information in countries other than your country of residence, subject to applicable safeguards and privacy requirements.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, advertising integrations, legal requirements, or information practices.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
