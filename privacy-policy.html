Privacy Policy for Bill Splitter

Last updated: 16 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bill Splitter, developed by Zenvora Labs, handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Bill Splitter does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with Zenvora Labs or third parties.

Information you enter, such as participant names, group names, expense descriptions, amounts, currency preferences, and settlement information, is stored locally on your device for the app's functionality.

2. How Your Data Is Used

Data entered in Bill Splitter is used only to calculate shared expenses, display balances and settlements, and save your app preferences and history on your device.

3. Data Sharing

Bill Splitter does not send your locally stored expense data to Zenvora Labs and does not share it with advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

If you choose to copy or share a settlement summary, the information is shared only through the app or service that you personally choose on your device.

4. Payments and Financial Services

Bill Splitter is a calculation and record-keeping utility. It does not process payments, transfer money, connect to bank accounts, provide loans, investment services, financial advice, or operate as a digital wallet.

5. Permissions

The current version does not require access to contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, bank accounts, or payment credentials for its core functionality.

6. Data Storage and Security

App data is stored locally on your device. Zenvora Labs does not operate a server that receives your expense data in the current version.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device. Device backup or restore features provided by your operating system may affect how locally stored app data is retained.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete the relevant groups or expenses, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Depending on your device backup settings, some data may also be retained by your device backup provider.

8. Children's Privacy

Bill Splitter is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Third-Party Services

The current version does not include advertising, analytics, account login, or cloud synchronization services. If this changes in a future release, this Privacy Policy and the app's Google Play Data safety disclosures will be updated accordingly.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at this same page with a revised “Last updated” date.
